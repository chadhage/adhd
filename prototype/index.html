              THE SHORT VERSION
              
    
              An idea is easier with a team.
              ADHD means Agent Driven Hybrid Development: people set the direction, AI helpers contribute, and results still need evidence.
              
    
              Factory = delivery coordinator
              A Factory brings the right Squads together for one product. Multiple Factories still share the same backlog and roadmap.
              Squad = small builder team
              Each Squad shares one board with two columns: To Do and In Progress. Builders tackle ready work and verify it together.
              
    
              First design the blueprint. Then configure a container Factory and its operating mandate, or connect a service for one manual card. Planned teams are distinct from observed running agents.
              
                
  
    
              YOUR COMFORT ZONE MATTERS
              
    
              Same service. Three places to run it.
              Choose hosted, local, or self-hosted execution. Planning works without a service; each execution path needs the listed real prerequisites.
              Hosted instances require an owner and deployment. Local services stay on loopback. Self-hosted instances require protected infrastructure and TLS. Tokens are never embedded in the static site.
