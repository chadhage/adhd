    STEP 3 OF 3

    A little team with big potential.

    A Factory coordinates delivery. Its Squads are small teams of AI builders.

    The starter blueprint proposes one Squad of three builders. Counts describe a plan, not running agents, approved WIP, or guaranteed parallel capacity.

    Prototype limits: 4 Factories, 4 Squads per Factory, 12 builders per Squad. These are preview limits, not framework policies. Each Squad shares one work board.

    How do the agents work together?

    A product guide keeps direction shared. Research helpers clarify needs. A work organizer (Kanban) keeps each Squad's board in order. Squad coordinators help builders work on ready tasks, then check the combined result. More builders alone do not mean more throughput.

          THE SHORT VERSION

          An idea is easier with a team.

          ADHD means Agent Driven Hybrid Development: people set the direction, AI helpers contribute, and results still need evidence.

          Factory = delivery coordinator

          A Factory brings the right Squads together for one product. Multiple Factories still share the same backlog and roadmap.

          Squad = small builder team

          Each Squad shares one board with two columns: To Do and In Progress. Builders tackle ready work and verify it together.

          You'll design a visual blueprint, not start live agents. The choice of runtime is separate from your product and team structure.
          YOUR COMFORT ZONE MATTERS

          Same framework. Different ways in.

          All five paths work in this prototype with a browser alone. These are the requirements for future real agent execution, not requirements for exploring this page.

          Only the VS Code + Copilot setup is documented in the current framework. Alternative adapters, hosted services, container packages, and CLI orchestration still need implementation and real-host validation. No credentials are collected here.
